# Email Analysis Checklist - SOC Analyst

## Step 1 - Check From Address
Is display name different from actual email address?
Is the domain legitimate or lookalike?

## Step 2 - Check Reply-To
Does Reply-To match the From address?
If different = replies go to attacker

## Step 3 - Check Authentication
SPF = pass or fail?
DKIM = pass or fail?
DMARC = pass or fail?
All three failing = confirmed suspicious

## Step 4 - Check Originating IP
Find X-Originating-IP in headers
Check on AbuseIPDB
Check on VirusTotal

## Step 5 - Check Domain
Check sending domain on VirusTotal
Check on MXToolbox blacklists

## Step 6 - Check URLs
Scan any links on URLScan.io
Never click directly

## Step 7 - Check Attachments
Upload to VirusTotal
Get hash first, check hash

## Step 8 - Build Verdict
Combine all findings
Document evidence
